# WHooF · What if phones had never been invented?  如果從來沒有發明電話？

**Channel:** WHooF — *Wonder, hear, observe, organize, find out.* A channel for curious minds exploring questions one step at a time.
**Format:** 55 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps. English voice (an anonymous kid), English subtitles with Traditional Chinese under them.
**Look:** stick figures in black marker on dotted notebook paper, one accent colour (orange). Lines draw on and "boil" at 12 fps.

## Three structures considered
1. **A list of "no phone" problems**: letters, maps, emergencies, one after another. Clear, but flat: no turn.
2. **Before / after split screen**: a day with phones vs. without. Strong contrast, but it's a comparison, not a question being explored.
3. **The WHooF method as the story** *(chosen)*: the kid answers the question by going through the channel's five steps (Wonder → Hear → Observe → Organize → Find out). Each step is one panel on a big notebook page. The camera travels panel to panel, then pulls back to show the whole page: the method *is* the answer. The same frame works for every future episode.

## Logline and arc
A kid's phone vanishes in a scribble, so they set out to find out what the world would be like without one, and end up seeing their family look up from the table.
- **Setup:** the question; the phone is scribbled out of the kid's hand.
- **Turn:** "Find out": phones only arrived in 1876; people managed with letters and pigeons.
- **Ending:** slower, but people look up and talk; "What do you think?" → full-page pull-back → WHooF logo.

## Benchmark (grammar only)
Hand-drawn explainer animation and picture-book pacing: one idea per frame, drawing *is* the action, a camera that travels across one continuous page. Not taken: any character designs, music, layouts or logos.

## Shot list
| # | Step | Framing / move | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wonder | close on the phone being drawn → pull back to full figure; phone scribbled out, "?" | hook in the first second; the "what if" happens on screen |
| 2 | Hear | pan right along the page → slow push toward Grandma | travel = next step; push = listening |
| 3 | Observe | pull back from the phone as four uses pop out | the reveal is the content: a phone is many things |
| 4 | Organize | tilt down, locked on a two-column table | organizing needs a still frame |
| 5 | Find out | hold on 1876, then truck left "back in time" to letters + pigeon | camera move = time travel |
| 6 | (answer) | pan left, slow push on the family at the table | the emotional landing |
| 7 | outro | pull back to the whole page → WHooF end card | the five steps seen together; brand |

## Sound
- **Silence → music:** no music until the phone goes *poof* (≈4 s); the uke comes in on that beat.
- **Held breath:** music drops out at "Find out" and comes back on the **1876** chime.
- **Score:** 100 bpm, I–V–vi–IV ukulele strum, plucked bass, shaker; glockenspiel notes light each WHooF letter; a recorder tune on the pull-back; final chord on the logo.
- **Foley:** marker squeak while lines draw, pops on reveals, scribble + poof, paper flips (days), door knocks, pigeon coo and wings, camera click, whooshes on camera moves.
- **Voice:** ~13 dB over the ducked music.
