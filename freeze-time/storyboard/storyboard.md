# Storyboard: “What If I Could Freeze Time Whenever I Wanted?”

Format: 9:16 vertical, 57 seconds, low-poly 3D with blocky characters, flat colors, soft shadows, and cinematic camera moves.

Number that ticks the whole video: a HUD at the top of the frame reads TIME BANK: 30.0s. Every second the world is frozen, the bank drains at exactly 1 second per second. It drains only during freezes, so the viewer always sees the cost.

Audio: kid’s voiceover, lo-fi chill beat with vinyl crackle that drops out during freezes, and a wind sound under the outdoor scenes (beats 1, 5, 6, 7 and 8).

Freeze windows (bank drains 1:1 inside each one):

| Beat | Beat time | Freeze | Length | Bank after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2s | none (rain falls normally) | 0 | 30.0 |
| 2 | 2–8s | 3.0–5.0s | 2s | 28.0 |
| 3 | 8–15s | 9.0–12.0s | 3s | 25.0 |
| 4 | 15–22s | 16.0–20.0s | 4s | 21.0 |
| 5 | 22–30s | 24.0–29.0s | 5s | 16.0 |
| 6 | 30–40s | 33.0–39.0s | 6s | 10.0 |
| 7 | 40–48s | 41.0–46.0s | 5s | 5.0 |
| 8 | 48–57s | 50.0–55.0s | 5s | 0.0 |

The freeze in Beat 7 ends at 46.0s, but the world does not start again: it stays locked (no drain, music still out) until 48.0s. After the bank hits 0.0 at 55.0s, the frozen final card holds until 57.0s.

## Beat 1 · 0–2s · The Hook

* Visual: Dusk on a low-poly hill. A blocky kid stands above a low-poly town on a flat-colored ground. Rain falls normally.
* Caption: What if I could freeze time whenever I wanted?
* HUD: TIME BANK 30.0s
* VO: “I found out I could.”
* Camera: Slow push-in.

## Beat 2 · 2–8s · The First Freeze

* Visual: The kid snaps their fingers at 3.0s. Rain drops, a kite, and a bird all stop mid-air. Camera orbits the frozen scene. The world starts again at 5.0s.
* Sound: Lo-fi beat cuts to silence, then a single vinyl crackle.
* Caption: I pause the world. It costs me 1 second per second.
* HUD: Bank ticks down to 28.0s.

## Beat 3 · 8–15s · Step One: Cookies (fun)

* Visual: The kid walks through frozen rain and takes a cookie from a jar on a shelf.
* Caption: Step one: cookies. Harmless, right?
* HUD: 25.0s.

## Beat 4 · 15–22s · Step Two: The Test (worse)

* Visual: A classroom. The kid freezes the teacher mid-write on the board and copies answers. The teacher’s marker hangs in the air.
* VO: “Nobody noticed. Nobody ever notices.”
* Caption: Step two: freeze the teacher to cheat.
* HUD: 21.0s.

## Beat 5 · 22–30s · Step Three: The Animals (worse again)

* Visual: A low-poly bird is frozen mid-flight over a pond. The kid freezes a dog mid-leap and forgets about it. The dog’s ball stays suspended.
* Music: Beat slows and gets heavier in the 22–24s gap, before the freeze.
* Caption: Step three: things get stuck when I stop them.
* HUD: 16.0s.

## Beat 6 · 30–40s · Step Four: The Friend (much worse)

* Visual: A fight at a playground. The kid freezes time to escape, then a friend is frozen mid-reach, arm out, face scared. The kid runs, leaving the friend behind.
* VO: “I just wanted to get away.”
* Caption: Step four: I froze someone and left them there.
* HUD: 10.0s.

## Beat 7 · 40–48s · Step Five: The Lock (worst)

* Visual: The kid tries to unfreeze. Nothing moves. Camera pulls out to show the whole town frozen, the kid the only figure still moving. The camera holds on the pull-out after the freeze ends at 46.0s.
* Sound: Wind rises, the beat drops out entirely.
* Caption: I can’t stop. I don’t know how to stop.
* HUD: Drops below 10s and turns red, flashing. Ends at 5.0s.

## Beat 8 · 48–57s · The Ending

* Visual: The beat returns for a moment, then a last freeze at 50.0s. The bank hits 0.0 at 55.0s and the whole screen goes still. The kid’s silhouette is the only thing moving, slowly.
* VO: “Some moments are supposed to move.”
* Caption (final card): What if you couldn’t unfreeze it?
* HUD: TIME BANK: 0.0s (red, holds)
* Music: Beat returns, softly, then stops on the final note.
