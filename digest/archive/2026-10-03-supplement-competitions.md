# Supplement — 2026-10-03 — Contests for the 11-year-old

*Sent as a separate email the same day the family asked for it. From 2026-10-04
this runs as **Section 5** of the daily digest. Profile now confirmed: **two

## Three days: North Star Math Contests, round 1 is 6 October and free

Six online rounds across the school year, **JK through grade 8**, English and
French, open to students **in Canada and internationally**. First round of the
season **free for every grade**.

2026–27 schedule: **6 October 2026 (free)**, 10–11 Nov 2026, 12–13 Jan 2027,
16–17 Feb 2027, 13–14 Apr 2027, plus a final round.

> **Entry route, the part that matters:** schools register through a coordinator,
> but **parents can register an individual student directly**. No school needed.

<https://northstartests.com/> — free, online, three days away, needs nothing but a
parent with an email address. Also the cleanest way to learn whether the child
actually *enjoys* competition maths before spending anything.

## Caribou Contests is dead — do not sign up

> Caribou was the obvious recommendation (international, online, grades 2–12,
> explicitly open to homeschoolers, ~$24 CAD/yr). It **ceased all operations on 11
> August 2025** after the death of founder Dr Thomas Wolf. It is **still
> recommended all over the internet** on unupdated blogs and school lists. Not
> running in 2026–27.

## Math Kangaroo — Taiwan, 2027 registration open now

International version runs the **third Thursday of March**. Taiwan runs its own
organisation and **2027 registration is already open**. The child enters **Level
3** (grades 5–6): one **75-minute, 30-question paper, bilingual Chinese and
English**, at partner schools nationwide, online where a school applies.
Eligibility explicitly includes **"homeschooled equivalent"**. Best-established
route for a Taiwan-based 11-year-old because it is run locally.

> **Taiwan deadline and fee unverified** — family to confirm with the Taiwan
> organiser. Not guessed at here.

## Bow Seat Ocean Awareness Contest — free, worldwide, 11 is exactly the entry age

Ages **11–18 worldwide**, **free**, **Junior division 11–14**. Annual
ocean-conservation theme answered through visual art, creative writing, poetry or
spoken word, film, performing arts, or interactive/multimedia — rewards a child
who *makes things* rather than one who sits tests. Awards: Gold $1,000, Silver
$750, Bronze $500, Pearl $250, Honourable Mention $100. Requires an adult sponsor
and **a parent counts** — the most homeschool-compatible item found.
<https://bowseat.org/programs/ocean-awareness-contest/how-to-enter/>

> **Unverified:** aggregators give the 2027 deadline as **14 June 2027**; not
> confirmed against Bow Seat's own site. Treat as "sometime in June".

## Bebras — 9–20 November 2026, and Taiwan is among the world's largest fields

Computational-thinking puzzles online, **no programming required**, ages 6–18,
60+ countries each November. An 11-year-old sits **Benjamin**. Taiwan recorded
**168,126 participants** in the most recent round, so the infrastructure exists.

**The catch:** entry normally runs through a school registered with the national
organiser. For a homeschooling family the task is finding **Taiwan's
coordinator** and asking how an individual child sits it. Could not be
established from here, and the window is six weeks out — worth one email now.
<https://www.bebras.org/countries/>

## AMC 8 — eligible, 28 October deadline, and the blocker is not eligibility

Exam window **21–27 January 2027**. **Grade 8 or below, under 15.5** on exam day,
so the 11-year-old qualifies comfortably — and it is the entry to the **AMC/AIME
ladder** that matters for the elder child's applications. Fees charged to the
**site**, not the student: early bird **28 Oct 2026** ($55), regular 5 Jan 2027
($75), late 14 Jan 2027 ($115, returning managers only).

> **The real blocker:** the MAA registers **schools and test centres, not
> individual children**. US and Canadian homeschools can register as sites; from
> Taiwan it runs through **AMC International**, so the task is finding a
> participating host in Taiwan. 28 October is the cheap deadline.
> <https://maa.org/student-programs/amc/>
