### 團體自學 — THE CATEGORY ERROR, found 2026-10-21. READ THIS BEFORE ANY SECTION.

Taiwan's 非學校型態實驗教育 has **THREE** forms, not one: **個人** (individual),
**團體** (group) and **機構** (institution). The 團體 form is an **自學互助會**, a
mutual-aid self-learning association founded jointly by the parents of **THREE OR
MORE STUDENTS** and capped at **THIRTY people** in total.
**WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN ALMOST ANYTHING ELSE IN THIS FILE:** for five weeks
the digest treated this family as individual self-learners facing institutions
that register classes rather than students, while separately recording
"teammates" as the top obstacle. 團體自學 addresses both at once — a group is a
recognised 實驗教育 unit, plausibly with a 指導老師 and a roster, which is what
Bebras, Conrad, the Ethics Olympiad and the World Scholar's Cup each ask for in
different words. **STOP ASKING ORGANISERS "can an individual self-learner
register?" and START ASKING "what do you accept from a 團體自學 group?"**
NOT VERIFIED, and must not be asserted: how any organiser actually treats a 團體;
whether forming one is practical or worth the administrative cost for this
family; the county application calendar for 團體自學.

### Section 1 findings logged 2026-10-16

**PHYSOLY IS COMMUNITY-BUILT — disclose it the way Omega Learn is disclosed.**
https://physoly.tech/ and https://physoly.tech/kalda/ — a free hub of notes,

### Section 6 — iNaturalist: THE LICENCE GATES GBIF ENTIRELY, established 2026-11-06

From iNaturalist's own guidance and forums:

- An observation becomes **research grade** when the community agrees on the ID.
- Research-grade observations are bulk-exported to **GBIF** roughly **weekly**.
- **Only if the licence permits.** CC0, CC BY and CC BY-NC are exported. **All rights
  reserved is excluded.** And — counter-intuitively — **CC BY-SA and CC BY-ND are
  ALSO excluded, because GBIF cannot process them.**
- Under CC BY, reusers must **credit the observer**.
- GBIF's "Recorded by" filter on the iNaturalist dataset uses the observer's
  **profile name**, not a user ID.

**THE ACTIONABLE FORM: this is a one-minute settings check, not a decision about
whether to participate.** A child can reach research grade on hundreds of
observations across a year and contribute nothing to any database if the licence is
left on all rights reserved — or on a Creative Commons licence that *sounds* more
generous and is in fact excluded. **CC BY both shares the data and compels
attribution.** Nobody tells you this.

**LIMITS, open:** no minimum age or account-age rule found for iNaturalist, exactly
as for eBird. The claim that citing a GBIF DOI discharges the attribution obligation
is a **forum post, not policy** — do not rely on it. Being in a database is not being
cited: most observations never are.

### STANDING INSIGHT, 2026-10-31 — A RATION IS NOT A LIMIT

The 2026 Hsinchu restriction gave its REASON: capacity. That distinction is now a
tool to apply everywhere in this file.

**A LIMIT** ("no one over fifteen", "accredited US high school", GitHub's age 13) is
a rule about what you ARE. Asking changes nothing.
**A RATION** ("only local students, because we have run out of places") is a rule
about SUPPLY. Rations have edges, waiting lists, and exceptions when a place opens.

They look identical from outside. **So the enquiry template changes: ASK WHY THE
RESTRICTION EXISTS, not merely whether it applies.** A reason identifies the kind of
rule; an exception request only reveals one person's mood. Apply this to the TRPEC
在學學生 call, to the WSC message, and to the IASC gate (**retracted 2026-11-12 — see
the correction block; the ask is now a registration form emailed to

the least interesting clause in a sentence, so it is the first thing cut when the

**THE RULE, and it is mechanical rather than a matter of care:**
